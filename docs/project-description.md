# Didilang Project Description

## Short Description

Didilang is a local discovery platform that helps people find nearby places such as food spots, barbershops, laundry shops, stores, and other useful establishments in their area.

## Full Description

Didilang helps communities discover local places faster and more accurately. Users can search for nearby establishments, view places on a map, check basic details, see ratings, and choose between satellite view or a default map with road and address labels.

The platform also allows people to submit new places they know. Each submission includes the place name, category, address or landmark, rating, photo, exact map location, and contact details for review. Submitted places are reviewed first before appearing publicly, helping keep the map useful, clean, and trustworthy.

Didilang is built for everyday local discovery. It is useful for residents looking for convenient nearby services, visitors exploring an area, and small local businesses that need better visibility.

## Problem It Solves

Many useful local places are hard to find online, especially small businesses that do not have strong online presence. People often rely on word of mouth, social media posts, or manual searching just to find nearby shops and services.

Didilang solves this by giving users one simple place to search, browse, and contribute local establishments based on real map locations.

## Target Users

- Residents looking for nearby food, services, and shops
- Visitors who need quick local recommendations
- Small business owners who want more visibility
- Community members who want to add useful places to the map
- Local admins or owners who want to review and manage submitted places

## Core Value

Didilang makes local discovery faster, more accurate, and more community-driven. It turns scattered local knowledge into an organized map of useful places.

## Product Positioning

Didilang is a practical community map for finding and sharing nearby local places. It focuses on real-world usefulness, simple discovery, and local business visibility.

## One-Line Pitch

Didilang helps people find and submit nearby local places through a simple community-powered map.

## Longer Pitch

Didilang is a community-powered local discovery platform where users can find nearby establishments, view them on a map, and submit new places for review. It helps residents and visitors discover useful local spots while giving small businesses better visibility in their area.

## Business Potential

Didilang can grow into a local discovery and business visibility platform. Possible revenue opportunities include promoted listings, verified business profiles, local advertising, premium placement for businesses, and tools for community or local area management.

## Brutal Feedback

The project is useful, but it needs strong local data to become valuable. The biggest risk is not the product idea itself, but whether enough accurate places are added and maintained. The fastest path to traction is to focus on one area first, fill it with high-quality listings, and prove that people actually use it to find nearby places.
