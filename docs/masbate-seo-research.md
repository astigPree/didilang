# Masbate SEO Research Notes

## Local Positioning

Didilang should be positioned as a community-powered local map for Masbate, Philippines. The clearest user intent is not broad tourism content. The strongest intent is practical discovery: people want to find nearby places, shops, food spots, services, landmarks, and exact locations in Masbate.

Masbate is strongly associated with:

- Rodeo culture and the "Rodeo Capital of the Philippines" positioning.
- Masbate City as the provincial capital and main commercial center.
- Local discovery across Masbate, Ticao, and Burias.
- Beaches, islands, small businesses, services, food spots, and community places.

Sources:

- Province of Masbate: https://masbate.gov.ph/
- Masbate festivals page: https://masbate.gov.ph/festivals/
- Official seal and island references: https://masbate.gov.ph/official-seal-and-symbolism/
- Philippine Statistics Authority PSGC Masbate listing: https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/citimuni/0504100000
- Google SEO Starter Guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide

## Target Search Intent

Primary search intent:

- Maghanap ng mga lugar sa Masbate
- Mga lugar sa Masbate
- Mapa ng Masbate
- Map sa Masbate
- Nasaan ang mga lugar sa Masbate
- Kainan sa Masbate
- Barberya sa Masbate
- Laundry sa Masbate
- Tindahan sa Masbate
- Masbate looking

Avoid presenting the site as "Google Maps" because that can be misleading. Use "local map", "mapa ng Masbate", and "map sa Masbate" instead.

## Brutal Ranking Reality

No implementation can guarantee that Google will prioritize the site. Google explicitly says there are no automatic first-rank secrets. Ranking will depend on content quality, crawlability, authority, links, user behavior, and whether the site provides better answers than existing results.

The fastest practical path is:

1. Fill the map with accurate Masbate places.
2. Add category pages later, such as `/masbate/kainan/`, `/masbate/barberya/`, and `/masbate/laundry/`.
3. Get local backlinks from Facebook pages, local businesses, tourism pages, schools, barangay pages, and community groups.
4. Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
5. Keep listings updated and remove low-quality or duplicate submissions.
